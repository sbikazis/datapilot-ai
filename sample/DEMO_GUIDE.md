# Sample report — how to read this demonstration

<escape>**This is a fictional demonstration, not a real customer report.**</escape>

The report below shows the style of output DataPilot AI aims to deliver: a compact executive overview, a transparent definition of net sales, month-by-month movement, category comparisons and explicit limitations.

## Open the sample files

- [Open the executive HTML report](SALES_SAMPLE_REPORT.html)
- [Inspect the exact synthetic CSV used to calculate it](synthetic_orders.csv)

The CSV contains 24 fictional order records covering January–June 2026. All values are invented for demonstration.

## What the sample demonstrates

- Gross sales, discounts, refunds and net sales are kept distinct.
- In this sample only, net sales = gross sales − discounts − refunds.
- Product category and month comparisons are shown only because those fields exist in the synthetic source.
- The report explains missing analytical scope instead of inventing metrics.

## Important limitations

- The upward month-to-month pattern was intentionally generated. It is not a benchmark, forecast or expected result.
- No real store, customer, order or transaction is represented.
- There is no reliable cost field, so the sample does not claim profit or margin.
- It does not claim customer lifetime value, inventory performance or advertising ROI.
- A real engagement may contain fewer sections—or different ones—depending on data quality and definitions.
