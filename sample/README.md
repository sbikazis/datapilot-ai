# DataPilot AI — Synthetic sales report sample

This folder contains a customer-facing demonstration asset built entirely from fictional data.

- `SALES_SAMPLE_REPORT.html`: self-contained executive report; open in any modern browser.
- `synthetic_orders.csv`: the exact fictional order-level source used to calculate the report.
- All amounts are explicitly USD. Net sales = gross sales − discounts − refunds.
- No real store, customer, transaction, benchmark, testimonial, or business outcome is represented.
- The monthly upward movement is intentionally generated and must not be presented as a forecast or expected customer result.
- The report does not calculate profit, margin, customer lifetime value, inventory or advertising ROI.

The sample is a marketing demonstration, not a claim that DataPilot has served a paying customer.
