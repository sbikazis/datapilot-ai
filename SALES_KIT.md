# DataPilot AI — Customer-facing sales kit

This document is the source of truth for the first paid-pilot positioning and outreach. It is intentionally narrow: one sanitized sales export, one human-reviewed report, one fixed introductory price.

## One-sentence positioning

DataPilot AI turns a small ecommerce business's existing CSV/Excel sales export into a clear, human-reviewed report showing what the data supports, what needs checking and what to investigate next.

## Offer at a glance

- **Who it is for:** small ecommerce operators and Shopify agencies whose clients already export order/sales data and still assemble some reporting manually.
- **Input:** one sanitized CSV or XLSX export.
- **Output:** one executive HTML report plus one XLSX workbook.
- **Included:** data-quality notes, supported sales metrics, useful product/category/time comparisons, evidence-backed observations and follow-up questions.
- **Introductory test price:** US$49 for one dataset / one report. Not yet validated by a paying customer.
- **Delivery:** agreed individually after reviewing the scope and data definitions.
- **Privacy:** customer removes personal/confidential information before sharing. No customer file is accepted before scope and payment arrangements are agreed.

## Objection handling

**“We already use Shopify Analytics.”**  
That may already cover your needs. The pilot is relevant only if you still have a specific reporting gap, need a review of an exported file, or want a simple client-ready workbook/report alongside your existing tools.

**“Can you calculate profit?”**  
Only if the export includes reliable cost data and we agree how costs, discounts, refunds and fees are defined. Otherwise the report stays with supported sales measures.

**“Can you connect directly to our store?”**  
Not in this pilot. The input is a customer-provided CSV/XLSX export.

**“Is the sample a real client?”**  
No. It is entirely synthetic and exists only to demonstrate report format and calculation transparency.

**“Can you send a recurring report automatically?”**  
Not currently. This is a one-off, manually reviewed pilot.

## Qualification before quoting

Ask only what is needed to confirm fit:
- What decision should the report help with?
- What does one row represent?
- Which date, sales, discount, refund and cost fields exist, and how are they defined?
- What date range and currency are covered?
- Can the file be sanitized before sharing?

## Proof and status language

Allowed: “fictional-data sample”, “introductory pilot offer”, “human-reviewed”, “metrics supported by the supplied file”.

Not allowed: “trusted by stores”, “proven results”, “case study”, “increased revenue”, “saved X hours”, “customer success” or “guaranteed insights” until independently true and documented.
